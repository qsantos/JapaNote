Quickly make Anki notes for Japanese words and sentences! Type a word below the deck list, in kanji, kana or romaji, even conjugated, and press Enter: JapaNote shows the note it would create, with the reading, furigana, definitions, pitch accent, an example sentence, the meanings and readings of the kanji, and tags for the JLPT level and common words. It tells when it is the dictionary form of what you typed, or already has a note. Press Enter again to add it, or edit it first: A recording by a native speaker, from Lingua Libre, is added to the note when there is one: When several words match, pick the one you want: To add the vocabulary of a lesson, paste the list in the field, or open a text or CSV file, then check the matches before adding them all at once: For a sentence, or anything not in the dictionary, JapaNote guesses the furigana. Proper nouns and technical words are found too, the latter on Wikipedia. The first time, choose the note type, and which field gets what. Leave out what you do not want: On first start, JapaNote downloads its dictionary (about 10 MB), which it then keeps up to date. It needs Anki 24.06 or later. Credits: JMdict, JMnedict and KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG), furigana from JmdictFurigana, JLPT levels from Jonathan Waller's lists, pitch accents from Kanjium, example sentences from Tatoeba, technical words from Wikipedia, recordings from Lingua Libre, and sentences analyzed with the IPA dictionary, as compiled by Janome.
